Seven ways to show project status

Each page is a working copy of how a well-known product shows progress, filled with the same made-up agency and clients.

Use the ‹ › arrows on the sides of the screen or in the top bar, or the ← → keys, to move between designs. The bar names each design and what inspired it. Click around: most things work.

To leave feedback, click Comments in the top bar. Comments are saved with the design and the screen you were on, and everyone with this link can read them.
